Arcade

Party games for one room. Everyone plays on their own phone, and nobody installs anything.

Scum

Get rid of your cards first and become President.

4 players, bots fill empty seats

4Color

Match the colour or the number. Don't forget to call your last card.

2–6 players, with optional bots

Undercover

Everyone knows the place except the spy. Ask questions and find them.

3–8 players, talking out loud

Investor

Buy streets, build houses, collect rent and trade your way to the top.

2–6 players, with optional bots

Nightfall

The Mafia strikes at night. By day, argue and vote them out.

4–12 players, talking out loud

Tic Tac Toe

Three in a row. Play a friend, or try to beat the bot.

2 players, or 1 against a bot

Drop Four

Drop discs and get four in a row before your friend does.

2 players, or 1 against a bot

Secret Hitler

Liberals against hidden Fascists. Elect governments, pass policies, and find Hitler.

5–10 players, talking out loud

Code Words

Two teams, one-word clues. Find your team's words and avoid the trap.

4 or more players, in two teams

How to play

  1. One person picks a game and presses Host.
  2. Everyone else scans the QR code on the host's phone.
  3. Type your name, join, and play.
